How Position and Z-Index Work

1. position: relative; The hero section acts as the positioning reference for its absolutely positioned decorative elements.

2. position: absolute; The decorative circle and floating card are positioned precisely inside the hero section.

3. z-index: 1; The decorative circle appears behind the text and card.

4. z-index: 2; The hero text appears above the decorative circle.

5. z-index: 3; The floating card appears above the circle and text layer.

The z-index property controls the stacking order of positioned elements within the relevant stacking context.

Conclusion

This assignment demonstrates how CSS positioning and z-index can create layered designs in a landing-page hero section. The decorative circle, main heading, and floating card are arranged at different layers to create a modern appearance.
