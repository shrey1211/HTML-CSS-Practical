How Flex-Wrap Works

1. display: flex; Arranges the product cards in a flexible row.

2. flex-wrap: wrap; Allows cards to move onto the next line when there is not enough horizontal space.

3. justify-content: center; Centres the cards within the available space.

4. gap: 25px; Adds space between the product cards.

5. Media query: Adjusts the product cards for smaller screens.
